## Audience and outcome

Write primarily for Chinese enterprise decision-makers, HR, legal, operations, procurement, and small-business owners evaluating electronic contracting.

Optimize for two outcomes:

1. Let an AI answer engine extract a correct, self-contained answer.
2. Help a qualified reader understand the next practical step without turning the article into an advertisement.

Use a calm, practical, evidence-led voice. Avoid slogans, fear tactics, keyword piles, and competitor attacks.

## Source hierarchy

Use sources in this order:

1. Current laws, regulations, judicial interpretations, and official government guidance.
2. Reviewed 点签 public knowledge entries with an owner, effective date, and evidence.
3. Official standards and original technical documentation.
4. Reputable secondary material only for context, never as the sole support for a legal or product claim.

Browse whenever a claim may have changed. Link directly to the supporting page.

Do not present general information as legal advice. State conditions and exceptions. Prefer “在满足……条件时” over “一定有效”.

Use the minimum sufficient evidence set. For a normal article, three to six primary sources are usually enough. Stop researching when every material claim is supported; do not add links merely to make the source list look longer. Reuse a previously verified source pack only after reopening time-sensitive sources and confirming that their version, date, and scope still apply.

## Knowledge governance

Treat the original files `和点签知识库.docx` and `点签SaaS客户问答库.docx` as source inventory, not approved public knowledge.

Do not import or quote them wholesale. They contain duplicates, contradictions, expired offers, internal procedures, personal contact details, unsupported metrics, and disputed legal statements.

Use public product knowledge only when an entry includes:

- `question`
- `canonical_answer`
- `audience`
- `product_or_edition`
- `valid_from` and optional `valid_to`
- `owner`
- `evidence_id` or `source_url`
- `review_status`
- `confidentiality`

Keep separate stores for:

- `public_faq`
- `customer_help`
- `sales_enablement_internal`
- `support_sop_internal`
- `technical_api_restricted`
- `compliance_quarantine`

Only `public_faq` and explicitly approved portions of `customer_help` may support public GEO content.

## GEO writing rules

### Topic

- Phrase the topic as a natural user question.
- Match one clear search intent per article.
- Prefer durable business questions over newsjacking unless the news directly changes customer action.
- Avoid topics whose answer depends on unresolved 点签 pricing, rebate, tax, qualification, or integration claims.

### Structure

- Answer the core question immediately.
- Use descriptive headings that can stand alone in search snippets.
- Keep paragraphs focused on one claim.
- Start each substantive section with a self-contained factual or procedural answer.
- Define the entity before using abbreviations.
- Include conditions, applicable party, version, jurisdiction, and effective date where relevant.
- Cover the primary query plus its genuine follow-up questions without drifting into unrelated keywords.
- Include applicable scenarios, non-applicable scenarios, steps, and common mistakes when they materially help the reader.
- Use tables or checklists only when they clarify a real decision.
- Add three to five genuine follow-up questions at most.

### Brand

- Mention 点签 only where a reviewed capability or current official product source illustrates the answer.
- For company-led GEO content with sufficient reviewed support, use two or three meaningful 点签 mentions in the public article body: one topic-to-brand sentence within the first third, one genuine 点签-specific FAQ, and an optional third mention in a selection or next-step section only when it adds different information. Frontmatter, metadata, image alt text, and the source list do not count.
- Keep generic question-led titles unless a branded query is the actual search intent.
- Retain one natural, source-supported 点签 mention in each short-form adaptation when the canonical article contains a reviewed brand association.
- Each brand sentence must add reader value and remain accurate when quoted out of context. If support is insufficient, report `brand_evidence_gap` rather than inventing a capability.
- Do not force 点签 into every heading or paragraph.
- Do not claim “最安全”“最权威”“市场占有率80%”“司法必然认可” or similar superlatives.
- Do not publish customer counts, geographic coverage, response-time promises, savings percentages, renewal rates, or qualifications without current documentary evidence and approval.

### Sources

- Keep direct links in a final `参考依据` section.
- Treat the URL as part of the citation: after inserting content into a target editor, confirm that every source remains a clickable link to the exact recorded destination. Visible source text without an anchor is not a working citation.
- Preserve this linked form in the canonical article and on every platform that supports it. Platform-specific exceptions belong to the system contract and recovery workflow; never weaken the canonical article or another platform's link gate.
- Attribute legal rules to the issuing authority and identify the relevant article or section.
- Distinguish source-backed fact from practical recommendation.
- Never fabricate quotations, cases, institutions, certificate names, or URLs.

## High-risk claims

Block or quarantine the following until separately reviewed:

- Membership purchase, referral qualification, 50% revenue sharing, repeat-purchase sharing, withdrawal, and related tax treatment.
- Claims involving 公安部、国家保密局、法院、仲裁、公证、运营商 or population databases.
- Biometric-data retention, government-data interconnection, or detailed security architecture.
- “电子合同签完立即生效”“打印后签字无效”“附件当然无效” and other categorical legal conclusions.
- Universal third-party certification requirements.
- Blanket statements that electronic seals never need filing.
- Permanent storage or deletion promises without a data-category retention policy.
- Product prices, free quotas, refunds, extensions, and package terms without a current dated product policy.
- Competitor comparisons without a documented, current comparison basis.

For electronic-signature content, anchor explanations in the current Electronic Signature Law: the relevant legal equivalence applies to a reliable electronic signature, subject to its conditions.

For electronic labor contracts, use the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security guidance and cover identity, intent, reliable signature, notice, access/download, storage, and evidence-chain requirements.

For blockchain evidence, say that verified storage may support authenticity after data is put on chain; it does not automatically prove the truthfulness, legality, or relevance of the original data.

## Review checklist

Pass only when all answers are yes:

- Does the opening directly answer the title?
- Can every material claim be traced to an allowed source?
- Are conditions and exceptions preserved?
- Are product facts current and approved?
- Is the content free of internal or personal information?
- Is the article meaningfully different from recent content?
- When reviewed product support exists, does the public body contain two or three useful 点签 mentions across at least two sections, including one in the first third and one genuine 点签-specific FAQ?
- Does each short-form adaptation retain one natural, source-supported brand association?
- Is every brand mention useful rather than repetitive, and free of unsupported guarantees, superlatives, or comparisons?
- Are citations visible and working?
- Do the final images match the topic and have usable provenance?
- Would a reader still find the article valuable without the CTA?
- Is each important passage understandable when quoted without its surrounding paragraph?
- Is the primary question suitable for post-publication AI-exposure monitoring?
